### This is the first generation of the NeuroMark templates.
It is crafted from a modelorder of 100 and consists of 53 Intrinsic Connectivity Networks (ICNs). Downloads are available from here, [Neuromark_fMRI_1.0 network template](https://github.com/trendscenter/gift/raw/master/GroupICAT/icatb/icatb_templates/Neuromark_fMRI_1.0.nii) and respective [network labels](https://trendscenter.org/trends/data/neuromark/Neuromark_fMRI_1.0_Labels.doc).

Calhoun, for the Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative, “NeuroMark: An automated and adaptive ICA based pipeline to identify reproducible fMRI markers of brain disorders” Neuroimage Clin, vol. 28, p. 102375, 2020, [PMC7509081.](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2213158220302126)
